Model Question Paper 1

Open each question to check the model answer. Try answering on your own before revealing it.

A. Choose the correct answer · 5 × 1 = 5
1. “The people who sat in darkness have seen a great light” — who is the Apostle who testified that these words of the prophet Isaiah were fulfilled in Jesus?

Answer box: St. James · John · Balaam · Moses · St. Matthew

Answer: St. Matthew.
2. “Did I not tell you that if you believed, you would see the glory of God?” — to whom did Jesus say this?

Answer box: To Martha

Answer: To Martha.
3. “I will raise up for them a prophet like you from among their brothers” — to whom did God make this promise?

Answer box: Moses

Answer: Moses.
4. Which feast does the Church celebrate on the Fridays of the Denha (Epiphany) season?

Answer box: Feast of All Saints

Answer: Feast of All Saints.
5. Through which encyclical did Pope Francis state that “this earth is our common home”?

Answer box: Laudato Si’

Answer: Laudato Si’.
B. Categorise and fill in the blanks · 3 marks
6. Jesus fulfilled His mission by doing five things for the integral liberation of humanity. Write them in order.

Word box: Good news to the poor · Liberty to the oppressed · Recovery of sight to the blind · The acceptable year of the Lord · Release to the captives

Answer in order:
  1. Good news to the poor
  2. Release to the captives
  3. Recovery of sight to the blind
  4. Liberty to the oppressed
  5. The acceptable year of the Lord
C. Match the following · 1 × 2 = 2
7. Subara
Answer: Sunrise (Suryodhayam).
8. January 6
Answer: Feast of Denha (Epiphany). It is also referred to in the answer box as Rakuli Perunnal.
D. Answer the following · 1 × 3 = 3
9. Write the 5th Beatitude.
Answer: “Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy.”
10. Complete the scripture verse: 1 John 3:18 — “Dear children, ______.”
Answer: “Dear children, let us not love with words or speech but with actions and in truth.”
11. Who is the patron saint of parish priests?
Answer: St. John Vianney.
E. Short answer · 1 mark
12. What is the message of the Parable of the Lost Sheep?
Model answer: God loves every person and does not abandon anyone who is lost. He seeks the lost sinner and rejoices when that person returns to Him.
F. Short paragraph · 2 marks
13. What is Global Warming? Briefly describe its causes.
Model answer: Global warming is the gradual increase in the Earth’s average temperature. It is mainly caused by the build-up of greenhouse gases due to burning fossil fuels, pollution, cutting down forests and other human activities.
