Tomorrow’s English Test Prep

Learn it step by step, look at a model, then try a scaffolded test. Take your time, Sam!

Test topics: ✏️ Editing a passage · 💌 Preparing an invitation card · ☎️ Telephonic conversation

These are writing-skill practice topics linked to the English mid-term study context (Mousetronaut and Six Dots). The exact questions may be different in the test.

1. ✏️ Editing a Passage

Read carefully. Find mistakes in capital letters, spelling, punctuation and grammar. Correct only what is wrong.

Quick checklist

  1. Check capital letters at the beginning of sentences and for names.
  2. Check full stops, commas, question marks and apostrophes.
  3. Check verb forms: he goes, not “he go”.
  4. Check singular/plural: the children are.
  5. Read the corrected passage again.
Wrong: sam and his sister goes to the library they reads a story.
Correct: Sam and his sister go to the library. They read a story.

Try one together

Wrong passage: last sunday, raju visit the zoo he see two elephants.

Model correction: Last Sunday, Raju visited the zoo. He saw two elephants.

Remember: keep the meaning the same while fixing the mistakes.

2. 💌 Prepare an Invitation Card

An invitation card tells someone about an event and invites them to attend.

Include these details

  1. What is the event?
  2. Who is inviting?
  3. Date and time
  4. Venue / place
  5. A friendly invitation line
  6. Name of the person inviting
🎉 YOU ARE INVITED! 🎉
Sam’s Birthday Party
Dear Friends,
Please join me to celebrate my birthday.
Date: Saturday, 7 November
Time: 4:00 p.m.
Venue: My home
Hope to see you there!
With love,
Sam

Sentence starters

“You are warmly invited to…”

“Please join us for…”

“We look forward to seeing you.”

Make it neat. You may decorate it, but do not forget the event, date, time, venue and name.

3. ☎️ Telephonic Conversation

A telephone conversation is a short dialogue. Use polite words and take turns speaking.

Remember this order

  1. Greet the person.
  2. Say your name.
  3. Ask for the person politely.
  4. Explain why you called.
  5. Reply politely and say goodbye.
Sam: Hello, may I speak to Arun, please?
Arun: Speaking. Who is calling?
Sam: Hi, Arun. This is Sam. Are you coming to the library today?
Arun: Yes, I will be there at four o’clock.
Sam: Great! Thank you. See you then.
Arun: You’re welcome. Goodbye!
Sam: Goodbye!

Useful phrases

“Hello, this is …” · “May I speak to …, please?” · “Could you please …?” · “Thank you for calling.” · “Sorry, he/she is not available.”

Write the speaker’s name followed by a colon. Keep each turn short and natural.

🌟 Before You Start the Test

  • Read the whole question once.
  • Underline the important instruction.
  • For editing, check one kind of mistake at a time.
  • For an invitation, use a clear layout.
  • For a dialogue, write both speakers’ names.
  • Save 2 minutes to check spelling and punctuation.
You do not need to write a perfect answer immediately. Complete one step, then the next.

📝 Practice Test Paper

Suggested time: 25–30 minutes · Total: 15 marks

Scaffolded practice: the clues help you begin. Try to complete the answers yourself before checking them.

Part A • Editing • 5 marks

Q1. Rewrite the passage correctly. Fix capital letters, verb forms and punctuation.

yesterday sam and his friend goes to the park they plays football and return home at six

Start: Yesterday, Sam and his friend…

Hints: goes → ______ · plays → ______ · Add a full stop after “park”.

Part B • Invitation Card • 5 marks

Q2. Prepare an invitation card for a school reading day.

Use these clues:

  • Event: School Reading Day
  • Date: 12 November
  • Time: 10:00 a.m.
  • Venue: School library
  • Invitees: Your classmates

Complete the card. You may use the sentence starters.

Part C • Telephonic Conversation • 5 marks

Q3. Your friend Anu calls to ask about the English homework. Complete the dialogue with suitable sentences.

Anu: Hello! May I speak to Sam, please?

Sam: Hello, this is Sam.

Anu: I want to know the English homework.

Sam:

Anu: Thank you for helping me.

Sam:

Anu: Goodbye!

Sam:

Helpful phrases: “How can I help you?” · “We have to…” · “You’re welcome.” · “Goodbye, Anu.”

Answer guide (check only after trying):

Q1: Yesterday, Sam and his friend went to the park. They played football and returned home at six.

Q2: Include “You are invited”, School Reading Day, Dear Classmates, a polite invitation, date, time, venue and your name.

Q3: Possible answer: “How can I help you?” / “We have to read the passage and answer the questions.” / “You’re welcome.” / “Goodbye, Anu.” Other sensible answers are acceptable.
