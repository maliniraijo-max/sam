🧩 Arithmetic of Parts

Fractions are pieces we can join, remove, compare and use to solve real problems. See the pieces first; then discover the calculation.

Chapter overview · the fraction toolkit

THE BIG IDEA
Same-sized parts
can be joined
or removed

Fractions describe how much of a whole we have. To add or subtract parts, first make sure the pieces are the same size.

🧠 Unlike fractions: make the pieces the same size

We will solve 1/4 + 2/8. Do not rush to add. First look at the size of each piece, then split the quarters into eighths, rewrite the fraction, and only then add.
