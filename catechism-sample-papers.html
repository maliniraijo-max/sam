Show model answer

E. Answer in a sentence

1. What is the significance of the Baptism of Jesus?

Show model answer
It reveals Jesus as the beloved Son of the Father and the Holy Spirit descending upon him; it begins his public mission.

2. How did Jesus overcome Satan, the tempter?

Show model answer
He resisted temptation by remaining faithful to God and answering with the Word of God.

3. What does St. Paul teach us about the Kingdom of God?

F. Essay (choose one)

1. How can we make the planet healthier and more sustainable?

Show model answer
We can protect our common home by planting and protecting trees, saving water and electricity, reducing plastic, reusing and recycling, keeping our surroundings clean, avoiding burning waste and protecting animals and habitats. Small actions by many people help future generations.

2. How does Jesus seek sinners and forgive them?

Show model answer
The Lost Sheep shows Jesus seeking the one who is lost; the Lost Coin shows the joy of finding what was lost; and the Prodigal Son shows a loving father welcoming a repentant child. These parables teach that God is merciful and rejoices when sinners return.

E. Answer in a sentence

1. What do we commemorate on Maundy Thursday?

Show model answer
The Last Supper, the institution of the Holy Eucharist and the priesthood, and Jesus washing the disciples’ feet.

2. What did Jesus pray on the Mount of Olives?

Show model answer
He prayed to the Father and surrendered to the Father’s will.

3. What does the Parable of the Lost Sheep reveal?

Show model answer
God seeks each lost person and rejoices when that person is found.

Show model answer

F. Essay (choose one)

1. Explain food safety from farm to table and the importance of home-cooked meals.

Show model answer
Food can become unsafe through contamination, adulteration and careless handling. We should wash hands and produce, use clean utensils, cook food properly, store it safely, check expiry dates and drink clean water. Home-cooked meals allow families to choose fresh ingredients, maintain hygiene and reduce excess salt, sugar and unhealthy additives.

2. How does Jesus seek sinners and forgive them?

Show model answer
The Lost Sheep, Lost Coin and Prodigal Son teach that God does not abandon those who are lost. He seeks them, welcomes sincere repentance and rejoices when they return. We are called to repent and show mercy to others.
