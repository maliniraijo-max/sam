Global Warming, Clean Air and Pure Food

The Earth is our common home, and we must protect it. Pope Francis explains the importance of caring for our common home in his encyclical Laudato Si’.

1. Global Warming: Global warming is the gradual rise in the Earth’s average temperature. Pollution, deforestation, and the burning of fossil fuels contribute to global warming. Planting more trees is a simple and effective way to help control it. We must protect forests and avoid activities that harm nature.

2. Clean Air: Clean air is essential for all living beings. Trees absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen, helping to maintain the balance of nature. They also help purify the air. Burning plastic produces harmful and toxic substances. Therefore, we should avoid burning plastic and reduce air pollution by planting more trees.

3. Pure Food: Pure and healthy food is essential for a healthy life. We should eat fresh fruits, vegetables, and nutritious food. We must avoid food contaminated with harmful chemicals and pesticides. We should wash fruits and vegetables properly before eating them and avoid wasting food. Growing vegetables organically and protecting our environment can help us obtain safe and healthy food.

Conclusion: The Earth is God’s precious gift to us. It is our duty to protect nature, plant trees, keep the air clean, and eat healthy food. Let us work together to protect our common home for future generations.
