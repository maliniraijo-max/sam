Chapter overview — your angle map

An angle tells us how far one ray turns away from another ray when they share the same starting point.

1. Parts of an angleVertex, two arms (rays), and the opening between them.
2. Compare anglesLook at the size of the opening, not the arm lengths.
3. Recognise typesLess than, equal to, or greater than a right angle.
4. Measure and drawUse a protractor carefully, aligning the centre and zero line.
5. Circles and turnsA full turn is 360°. Equal parts divide 360° equally.
6. Make patternsUse measured angles to construct shapes and repeated designs.
A full turn = 360°   ·   Half turn = 180°   ·   Quarter turn = 90°
