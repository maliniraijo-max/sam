Chapter 1 · Jesus Who Overcame the Three Temptations

Temptation, fasting, Lent and Holy Week

What were the three things Jesus was tempted for?
Sensuality (pleasure), authority, and wealth.
What strengthens us to overcome temptations?
The Word of God.
From where do we receive strength to conquer temptations?
From fasting, penance (abstinence), and prayer.
How did Western missionaries describe the St. Thomas Christians (Mar Thoma Nazranis)?
“Friends of Lent” (friends of fasting).
What does the word “Upavasam” (fasting) mean?
To dwell along with God—to live in communion with God.
Which is the fourth season in the liturgical year of the Syro-Malabar Church?
The Season of Lent (Nombukalam).
What is the fasting period before Easter called?
Great Lent (Valiya Nombu).
What is the first Sunday of the Great Lent called?
Pethurtha.
Which solemn service of repentance takes place at the beginning of Lent?
Ash Monday (Vibhuti Acharanam).
What is the final week of Lent called?
Holy Week (Great Week).
What do we commemorate on Palm Sunday (Oshana Sunday)?
Jesus’ triumphal entry into Jerusalem.
What do we commemorate on Maundy Thursday (Pesaha Thursday)?
The institution of the Holy Eucharist and the Priesthood.
What does the bread-breaking service at home on Maundy Thursday signify?
It represents Jesus’ Passover meal.
What events do we commemorate on Good Friday?
The passion, crucifixion, death, and burial of Jesus.

Chapter 4 · Jesus Proclaiming the Gospel of the Kingdom of God

Jesus’ mission, the poor, and God’s Kingdom

Which Holy Book was given to Jesus to read when He entered the synagogue?
The Book of the Prophet Isaiah.
Jesus came into the world to liberate us from all forms of what?
Bondage (slavery to sin and suffering).
What was the primary mission of Jesus?
Proclaiming the Kingdom of God.
Which three things of Jesus were good news to the poor?
His words, deeds, and presence.
Who are the “poor in spirit”?
Those who do not rely on material wealth, but depend on God’s mercy and providence.
In which pool did Jesus tell the blind man to wash?
The Pool of Siloam.
Why did God give laws to mankind?
For human well-being and salvation.
“The Kingdom of God is not food and drink, but …” What are the three things?
Righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit.
What is the community of people who fulfil God’s will called?
The Kingdom of God.
Which are the first four books of the New Testament?
The Gospels according to Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John.

Chapter 5 · Jesus Who Revealed Divine Glory

The wedding at Cana, calming the storm, healing and Lazarus

What was the chief drink at a Jewish wedding feast?
Wine.
What did the Blessed Virgin Mary say to Jesus at the wedding?
“They have no wine.”
What was Jesus’ first miracle that revealed His glory?
Changing water into wine at the wedding in Cana.
What did Jesus say to the sea after rebuking the wind?
“Peace! Be still!”
What should be with us so we do not shatter when difficulties arise?
Jesus.
What was the name of the pool near the Sheep Gate in Jerusalem?
Bethesda.
What did Jesus say to the paralyzed man at the pool?
“Stand up, take your mat and walk.”
What were the names of Lazarus’ sisters?
Martha and Mary.
What did Jesus say to Martha?
“Did I not tell you that if you believed, you would see the glory of God?” He also said, “I am the resurrection and the life.”
What did Jesus cry out after praying to the Father?
“Lazarus, come out!”

Chapter 6 · Jesus Who Came Seeking Sinners

Mercy, repentance and forgiveness

Which animal was considered most detestable by the Jews?
Pig.
Who was restored to his lost position of sonship?
The Prodigal Son.
Which parable illustrates God’s boundless mercy towards repentant sinners?
The Parable of the Prodigal Son.
Which parable did Jesus tell when Pharisees and scribes criticised Him for eating with sinners?
The Parable of the Lost Sheep.
Who said, “If this man were a prophet, he would have known who and what kind of woman this is who is touching him”?
A Pharisee named Simon.
What did Jesus say to those who wanted to stone the adulterous woman?
“Let anyone among you who is without sin be the first to throw a stone at her.”
What did Jesus say to the sinful woman?
“Neither do I condemn you. Go your way, and from now on do not sin again.”
To whom should we turn quickly if we fall into sin?
To Jesus.
Whose law commanded that such women should be stoned to death?
The Law of Moses.
What was Jesus doing when the sinful woman was brought to Him?
He was teaching the people in the temple courts.

Chapter 7 · Jesus Who Revealed the Suffering Servant

Jesus’ suffering, the bronze serpent, and salvation

Which prophet portrayed Jesus as the Suffering Servant?
Isaiah.
In whom was the figure of the Suffering Servant fulfilled?
Jesus.
Who sacrificed Himself for the sins of mankind?
Jesus.
What was placed on Jesus’ head?
A crown of thorns.
What was placed in Jesus’ hand?
A reed.
What caused wounds all over Jesus’ body?
Scourging with a whip.
Where did Jesus die?
On the Cross.
Who chose Jesus as the Suffering Servant?
The Father.
How many times did Jesus foretell His death?
Three times.
Who said, “God forbid it, Lord! This must never happen to you”?
Peter.
Who said, “Get behind me, Satan!”?
Jesus.
Where did Peter profess Jesus as the Christ?
Caesarea Philippi.
Who confessed Jesus as “the Son of the living God”?
Peter.
Which Gospel records the first prediction of Jesus’ passion?
The Gospel of Matthew.
Which three Evangelists recorded Jesus’ predictions of His passion?
Matthew, Mark, and Luke.
Which Evangelist presents Jesus’ passion through a symbol?
John.
What symbol did John use to represent Jesus being lifted up?
The serpent.
Who lifted up the serpent in the desert?
Moses.
What material was the serpent made from?
Bronze.
Where was the bronze serpent lifted up?
In the desert (wilderness).
What did the Israelites experience in the desert?
Serpent bites.
Who sent the poisonous (fiery) serpents among the people?
God.
Who made the bronze serpent?
Moses.
What did those who looked at the bronze serpent receive?
Life and healing.
Where was Jesus lifted up to save mankind?
On the Cross.
Where was Jesus crucified?
Calvary (Golgotha).
From what does Jesus save us?
From sin.
What do those who look to Jesus with hope and faith receive?
Life (eternal life).
Who will draw everyone to Himself when He is lifted up?
Jesus.
What should disciples do for those who suffer?
Comfort them.
Where should Christians comfort those who suffer?
In the community (society).
What did Jesus take upon Himself?
Our wounds (infirmities).
What did Jesus offer for our salvation?
Himself as a sacrifice.
For what did Jesus suffer?
For our sins.
What must we bear for the good of others?
Sufferings.
Which prophet spoke about the Suffering Servant?
Isaiah.
Which chapter states, “But he was wounded for our transgressions”?
Isaiah, Chapter 53.
In which book is Isaiah 53:5 found?
The Book of Isaiah.
Which Gospel compares Jesus’ crucifixion to the lifting of the bronze serpent?
The Gospel of John.
Which chapter talks about the lifting up of the serpent?
John, Chapter 3.
