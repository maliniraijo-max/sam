🍕 One Fraction Many Forms

Same amount, different-looking fractions. Explore the pieces, change the numbers, and discover why the value stays the same.

Chapter overview — your fraction map

A fraction describes equal parts of a whole. The numerator tells how many parts we count; the denominator tells how many equal parts make the whole. One fraction can be written in many forms without changing its value.

One half — the same amount in two ways
1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8
1. Fraction partsNumerator, denominator, equal parts, and fractions greater than one whole.
2. Equivalent fractionsMultiply numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number.
3. Lowest termsDivide numerator and denominator by common factors until no common factor greater than 1 remains.
4. Fractions as divisiona ÷ b = a/b when b is not zero; a fraction can describe sharing.
5. Mixed numbersAn improper fraction can be expressed as whole groups plus a fractional part.
6. Real sharing problemsShare ribbons, rods, or quantities equally and express the amount each person receives.
a/b = (a × k)/(b × k)   ·   a ÷ b = a/b
